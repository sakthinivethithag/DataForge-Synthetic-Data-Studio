What these checks mean

  • Exact overlap: generated records that match source rows across shared fields.
  • Repeated rows: duplicate records inside the generated candidate.
  • Rare values: simple count of generated values appearing once in a source column.
  • Identifier hints: column names that look like names, phone numbers, emails or addresses.

Recommendations: remove direct identifiers, generalize rare categories, reduce copying/perturbation fidelity, and consult a privacy specialist before releasing sensitive data.

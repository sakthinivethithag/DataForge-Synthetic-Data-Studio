Generation settings

Statistical sampling fits a normal distribution to numeric columns. Perturbed sampling adds bounded random noise to sampled numeric values.

Optional constraints

Apply bounds by matching the exact numeric column name. Example: age between 18 and 65.

How to interpret results

A synthetic dataset is newly sampled, not a renamed copy of the source. Statistical relationships may not be preserved by this baseline.

Always evaluate the generated output. Better distribution similarity does not guarantee privacy, fairness, or downstream model performance.
